The bars show the energy of every gate, the black mark is its value and the orange mark the highest energy since the page was opened. A red bar is above its value; the radar also filters, a gate just above its value does not always report a target. To find what a gate sees in an empty room, leave the page open for a while. "Raise values" sets every gate whose peak reached its value to the peak + 10, Save Changes writes them to the radar.

Signal strength a gate needs to report a target: Move starts the detection, Still keeps it on while the person does not move. The radar reports a target only when its energy (0-100) is above the value: a higher value makes a gate less sensitive, 100 ignores the gate. Raise the values of a gate that detects presence in an empty room (fans, curtains, plants in a draft, people behind a wall) and lower them if a person sitting still is lost. The radar stores the values itself, the changed gates are written to it when the form is saved. The serial console command AT+LD2410C=mon,30,gates prints the energy of every gate, AT+LD2410C=factory resets the module to its defaults
