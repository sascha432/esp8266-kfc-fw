X is left and right of the sensor (negative is left), Y is the distance in front of it. The radar reports up to three targets, a target with speed 0 is standing still. The serial console is paused while this page is open.
Installation
  1. Try to ensure that the radar antenna faces the area to be detected, and that the surroundings of the antenna are open and unobstructed.
  2. Ensure that the sensor installation position is firm and stable. Shaking of the radar itself will affect detection performance.
  3. Ensure that there are no moving or vibrating objects behind the radar. Since radar waves are penetrating, the back lobe of the antenna signal may detect moving objects behind the radar. A metal shield or metal backplate can be used to shield the radar back lobe and reduce the impact caused by objects behind the radar.
  4. When multiple 24GHz band radars are present, do not install them facing each other's beam direction. Keep installation positions as far apart as possible to avoid possible mutual interference.
