Area of a zone in millimeters: X is left and right of the sensor (negative is left), Y is the distance in front of it, (0, 0) is the sensor itself. A target inside the box X1..X2 and Y1..Y2 counts as inside. The three areas together are one zone: "Detection" reports the targets inside the areas, "Filter" hides them (mask a fan or a curtain). The radar stores the values itself, the changed values are written to it when the form is saved. The serial console command AT+LD2450=mon shows what the radar reports, AT+LD2450=zone,off disables the areas
Second area of the zone, ignored while all four values are 0. The areas do not have to be sorted, X1/Y1 is one corner and X2/Y2 the opposite one
Third area of the zone, ignored while all four values are 0
